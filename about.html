Our Journey

Founded in 2024, Coders Club started as a small group of passionate developers meeting in campus labs. Today, we're a thriving community of 500+ developers pushing the boundaries of technology and building real solutions for real problems.

From our first web project to enterprise-grade applications, every line of code has been written with purpose — to learn, to grow, and to make a difference.
